I love building things. As a Software Engineer throughout my years of career, I’ve had the opportunity to see complex
systems happen to exist in simple ways, and that has been the main motivation to continue building things. Or
improving existing things.

Ensuring the software is **designed to serve the needs, not vice versa**, has been one of the most interesting
challenges I’ve been addressing during the years. Such a simple concept yet you see it gets out of the room when a
system is being designed. This very simple approach alone has let me maintain the simplicity and usefulness of the
systems I’ve been working on.

Nowadays in the AI era, I also build out of curiosity and **this website is mostly to have them somewhere online**. I
build cool ideas just because they deserve to exist, and it’s the best time ever for them to have the chance to exist.
In my fun projects, **I don’t really care if they are interesting to others**. Like the saying, Dance like nobody is
watching.
